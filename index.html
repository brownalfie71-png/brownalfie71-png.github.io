Hi, I’m Alfie

I like objects that carry a story. My influences are the florals of the Arts and Crafts movement, brutalist posters and Asian postage stamps, and a lot of my work comes back to the Isle of Sheppey, where I grew up. I'm now in Brighton, studying a BSc in Product Design at the University of Sussex, and working across packaging, print and physical product.

Selected work

University brief · Schofield Watch Company

The Montgomery

A watch box styled as a crate 'recovered' from the SS Richard Montgomery, the WWII wreck off the Isle of Sheppey where I grew up. Fumed oak, steel corners, a cork interior, and a lid carving that shouldn't exist.

University brief · Bird & Blend Tea Co.

Tea Light

A plastic-free, refillable candle range for Bird & Blend, scented by steeping real tea into soy wax, with illustrated gift packaging for four blends.

University brief · Big Potato Games

GO CAPY!

Designed for a Big Potato Games brief, Go Capy! is a fast-paced dexterity card game with a physical capybara plush that keeps it chaotic and fun.

Final year project · In progress

STRUT Controller

Game console controllers are made to be discarded. This project designs a controller whose sticks, battery and shell can be replaced so it can be repaired instead.

Group project · Design for Manufacture

Balance Bike

How efficient can a balance bike be? We took one apart and redesigned it with 35 parts instead of 60, whilst keeping it safe and functional.

University project

The Bubble

A self checkout for libraries, paired with an accessible app that lets visually impaired users take out books in a format that suits their needs.

Personal project

Captured

A poster made while waiting for a secondhand Fujifilm camera to arrive, about feeling stuck in your home town. The photograph inside the letters is Sheerness.

University project

RunIt

An adjustable running belt that changes size and capacity to fit any runner, taken from sketches through to a physical prototype.
